Lead Magnets — Self-Assessment Surveys

Four Assessments Mapped to GEC Services

Strategy Consulting
Strategy Readiness

12-15 questions · 4 min · Strategy maturity scorecard

Business Process Management
BPM Maturity

10-12 questions · 3 min · Process maturity level + gap analysis

Export Consulting
Export Readiness

12-15 questions · 4 min · Market-readiness score + checklist

Digital Transformation / AI
AI Readiness

10-12 questions · 3 min · AI adoption roadmap preview

How Each Assessment Works

  1. User lands on assessment page (from ad, social post, event, email, or website)
  2. 10-15 questions, 3-5 minutes, progress bar visible
  3. Results are gated — "Enter your email to see your score"
  4. They immediately receive: on-screen score summary + PDF scorecard via email
  5. Behind the scenes: contact created in intelligence DB, score determines nurture track, Apollo.io enriches company data

Why This Solves the Conversion Time Problem

  • You already know their pain points before the first call
  • The assessment pre-educates them on why they need help
  • The PDF report creates perceived value before you quote anything
  • Score data auto-populates into proposals/diagnostics
  • Discovery calls are 50% shorter because you already know their situation

Section 08

AI-Powered Personalized Communication

The Principle

No two contacts receive the same communication. The AI decides:

  • What to send (insight, resource, invitation, or nothing)
  • When to send it (optimal timing for that person)
  • How to frame it (their communication style)
  • Whether to send at all (sometimes silence is better)

How It Works

FOR each contact, EVERY week: 1. GATHER context: - Last interaction date and type - Assessment data and scores - Recent company news (Apollo) - Email engagement patterns - Upcoming events matching their interests 2. AI EVALUATES and recommends ONE of: a. Send personalized insight ("I noticed your company just expanded to...") b. Share a relevant resource (template, guide, case study matching their weak area) c. Invite to upcoming event (only if it matches their interests) d. Do nothing (they're engaged enough, don't over-communicate) e. Alert Lia for personal outreach (high-value moment detected — job change, funding, etc.) 3. LOG the action → feeds back into the profile

What Makes This Not Aggressive

  • Triggered by their actions, not cold spam
  • Every touchpoint delivers genuine value
  • CTAs are always soft: "free diagnostic", "here's a resource"
  • Frequency adapts to engagement level
  • System knows when to stay quiet

What Makes This Not Boring

  • Content is personalized to their specific assessment results
  • Mix of formats: PDF, video, case study, checklist, invitation
  • Language matches preference (Georgian / English)
  • Each message has a "quick win" they can implement immediately
  • Timing and channel vary (not predictable patterns)
