## 1. Vision & Core Philosophy

### The Principle

```
MEET them  →  GIVE them something  →  SOLVE their problem  →  They spread the word
```

Every interaction with GEC should provide value. The system is designed so that once a potential client enters the ecosystem, they continuously receive useful insights, tools, and connections — never aggressive pitches, never boring templates.

### What We're Building

A fully integrated digital sales machine that:

- **Attracts** through automated content, partnerships, and live events
- **Captures** through self-assessments, webinars, and workshops
- **Nurtures** through AI-personalized communication (not generic drip sequences)
- **Qualifies** automatically through behavioral scoring and assessment data
- **Converts** by decreasing time-to-contract with pre-built diagnostic insights
- **Retains** through ongoing value delivery and community

### The Full Funnel

```
ATTRACT            CAPTURE           NURTURE             QUALIFY            CLOSE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Auto-posting  →  Lead Magnets   →  AI-personalized  →  Self-Assessment  →  Auto-Proposal
SEO content      (assessments)     email sequences     scoring + CRM       (templated)
Webinars         Gated PDFs        behavior-based      Pipeline auto-      Pre-filled
Workshops        Event signups     multi-channel        moves in CRM       contracts +
Partner events   Contact forms     value delivery       Alert to Lia       booking links
```

## 5. Lead Magnets — Self-Assessment Surveys

### Four Assessments Mapped to GEC Services

| Assessment | GEC Service | Questions | Time | Output |
|------------|------------|-----------|------|--------|
| **Strategy Readiness** | Strategy consulting | 12-15 | 4 min | Strategy maturity scorecard |
| **BPM Maturity** | Business Process Management | 10-12 | 3 min | Process maturity level + gap analysis |
| **Export Readiness** | Export consulting | 12-15 | 4 min | Market-readiness score + checklist |
| **AI Readiness** | Digital transformation / AI | 10-12 | 3 min | AI adoption roadmap preview |

### How Each Assessment Works

1. User lands on assessment page (from ad, social post, event, email, or website)
2. 10-15 questions, takes 3-5 minutes, progress bar visible
3. Results are **gated** — "Enter your email to see your score"
4. They immediately receive:
   - On-screen score summary
   - PDF scorecard via email (beautifully designed, shareable)
5. Behind the scenes:
   - Contact created/updated in intelligence DB
   - Score determines which nurture track they enter
   - Score pre-qualifies them (high score = warm lead, low score = needs education)
   - Assessment answers populate diagnostic preview for future proposals
   - Apollo.io enriches their company data automatically

### Why This Solves the Conversion Time Problem

- You already know their pain points before the first call
- The assessment pre-educates them on why they need help
- The PDF report creates perceived value before you quote anything
- Score data auto-populates into proposals/diagnostics
- Discovery calls are 50% shorter because you already know their situation

## 7. Contact Intelligence Layer

### What We Track Per Contact

#### Identity
- Name, email, phone
- Company, role, title
- LinkedIn profile URL
- Company size + revenue (Apollo enrichment)
- Industry + sector
- Location
- How they found us (UTM source)

#### Engagement History
- Webinars attended (which topics)
- Assessments taken (scores + individual answers)
- Emails opened/clicked (which content)
- Events attended (online + offline)
- Content downloaded
- Website visits + pages viewed
- Social interactions with GEC content

#### AI-Generated Intelligence
- Communication style preference
- Decision-making pattern (fast/slow, data-driven/intuition)
- Best time and channel to reach them
- Predicted needs based on behavior
- Lead temperature (hot/warm/cold)
- Risk of going cold
- Recommended next action
- Summary of all interactions (AI-written)

#### External Intelligence (Enriched)
- Job changes (LinkedIn monitoring)
- Company news and announcements
- Funding rounds
- Industry trends affecting them
- Competitor activity
- Public posts/articles they publish

#### Vector Embeddings
- All interactions embedded for semantic search
- Enable: "Find me leads similar to [best client]"
- Enable: "Which contacts are interested in [topic]?"
- Enable: "Who should I invite to [event about X]?"

### Company Intelligence

For each company in the system:
- Revenue, employee count, founding year
- Industry classification
- Digital maturity indicators
- Known pain points (from assessments + interactions)
- Key decision makers mapped
- Engagement timeline
- Opportunity score

## 8. AI-Powered Personalized Communication

### The Principle

No two contacts receive the same communication. The AI decides:
- **What** to send (insight, resource, invitation, or nothing)
- **When** to send it (based on optimal timing for that person)
- **How** to frame it (based on their communication style)
- **Whether** to send at all (sometimes silence is better than noise)

### How It Works

```
FOR each contact, EVERY week:

  1. GATHER context:
     - Last interaction date and type
     - Assessment data and scores
     - Recent company news (Apollo)
     - Email engagement patterns
     - Upcoming events matching their interests

  2. AI EVALUATES and recommends ONE of:
     a. Send personalized insight
        ("I noticed your company just expanded to...")
     b. Share a relevant resource
        (template, guide, case study matching their weak area)
     c. Invite to upcoming event
        (only if it matches their interests)
     d. Do nothing
        (they're engaged enough, don't over-communicate)
     e. Alert Lia for personal outreach
        (high-value moment detected — job change, funding, etc.)

  3. LOG the action → feeds back into the profile
```

### What Makes This Not Aggressive

- Triggered by **their** actions (assessment, event attendance), not cold spam
- Every touchpoint delivers genuine value
- CTAs are always soft: "free diagnostic", "here's a resource"
- Frequency adapts to engagement level (engaged = less outreach needed)
- System knows when to stay quiet

### What Makes This Not Boring

- Content is personalized to their specific assessment results
- Mix of formats: PDF, video, case study, checklist, invitation
- Language matches their preference (Georgian / English)
- Each message contains a "quick win" they can implement immediately
- Timing and channel vary (not predictable patterns)

## 9. Nurture Flow Design

### Post-Assessment Sequence (Adaptive)

| Timing | Action | Condition |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Day 0 | Assessment completed → PDF scorecard (instant) | Always |
| Day 1 | "Here's what your score means" email | Always |
| Day 3 | Micro-case study relevant to their weakest area | Score < 7/10 |
| Day 5 | Free resource (template, checklist, mini-guide) | Always |
| Day 8 | "Companies like yours typically..." (social proof) | Not yet engaged |
| Day 12 | Invite to free 20-min diagnostic call | Score indicates need |
| Day 15 | Personalized insight based on assessment data | Always |
| Day 20 | Exclusive content or webinar invite | If upcoming event matches |
| Day 30 | Check-in with new industry insight | Always |
| Day 30+ | Moves to AI-personalized weekly cadence | Always |

### Post-Event Sequence

| Timing | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Same day | "Thanks for attending" + recording link + slides |
| Day 2 | Key takeaway summary + "What did you find most useful?" (micro-survey) |
| Day 5 | Related assessment invitation (if not taken) |
| Day 10 | Related resource or next event invitation |

### Re-engagement (Going Cold)

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| No interaction for 30 days | Share an industry insight (no CTA) |
| No interaction for 60 days | "Quick question" email about their industry |
| No interaction for 90 days | Move to quarterly newsletter only |
