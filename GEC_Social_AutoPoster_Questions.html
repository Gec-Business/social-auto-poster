Client Management & Multi-Account

The system will serve multiple clients through one admin panel. We need to understand how client accounts should be separated and managed.
Q2.1

And realistically, how many could Tea handle with the automated system? (3 clients vs 20 is very different architecturally.)
Q2.2
Are all clients similar or do some have very different needs?
Some might need only Facebook, others need all platforms. Some might need reels, others only image posts. How much variation exists?
Q2.3
Who provides the brand guidelines for each client?
Colors, fonts, tone of voice, do's and don'ts — does each client give this to Tea? Is it documented or informal? The AI needs this to generate content that matches each brand.

Approval Workflow

We're planning a system where clients get a simple link + passcode to review and approve their monthly content. These questions define exactly how that works.
Q4.1
Monthly batch flow — does this sequence make sense?
  1. AI generates a month of posts for a client
  2. Tea reviews in her admin panel (edits, adjusts, regenerates as needed)
  3. Tea marks batch "ready for client review"
  4. Client receives a link + passcode, reviews all posts
  5. Client approves/rejects individual posts, leaves comments
  6. Tea addresses rejections, resubmits
  7. Once all approved, posts are locked and scheduled for auto-posting
Does this match how Tea would want to work? What would she change?
Q4.2
When a client rejects a post, what information does Tea need?
Just "rejected" or a comment explaining why? Should the client be able to suggest specific edits?
Q4.3
How many review rounds are realistic?
One round? Multiple until everyone is happy? Is there a deadline after which unapproved posts just don't get posted?
Q4.4
Should clients be able to see posting history / past months?
Q4.5
Should clients see engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares) for posted content?
