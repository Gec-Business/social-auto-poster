Executive Summary

We are building an AI-powered marketing platform that automates what GEC currently does manually — market research, competitor analysis, content strategy, and social media posting — and delivers it to multiple clients simultaneously.

The platform has exceptionally low operating costs (under $4 per client per month) while delivering high-value services priced at $49–$999/month per client. This means 90%+ profit margins at scale.

7 What Drives Costs Up? (And How We Control Them)
Understanding the main cost drivers and the levers we have to keep costs low.
Cost Driver Impact How We Control It
AI API Usage i Every time AI reads data and generates text, we pay per "token" (roughly per word). More complex analysis = more tokens = higher cost. This is ~80% of the per-client cost. HIGH Use a cheaper AI model for drafts and research (saves 60%). Cache common research data so similar clients share costs (saves 30%). Set monthly budget caps per client tier.
Database Storage i As we store more AI reports, content history, and analytics for more clients, the database grows. AI "embeddings" (used for smart search) are especially space-hungry at ~1KB each. MEDIUM Archive old data after 12 months. If storage costs rise above $69/mo, consider self-hosting the database on our existing VPS ($0 extra).
App Hosting (Vercel) LOW Free tier covers MVP. Pro tier ($20/mo) is a flat cost — doesn't increase with more clients.
Social Media APIs NONE Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn APIs are free. No cost increase regardless of client count.
Email Sending NONE Covered by existing Microsoft 365 license. Limit of 10,000 emails/day — far beyond our needs.
⚠
Worst-case scenario: Even if AI costs double and we don't optimize at all, the per-client cost would be ~$7/mo — still under 15% of the lowest pricing tier ($49). The business model has a wide safety margin.
